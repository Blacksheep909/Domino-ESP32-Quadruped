# Media Wishlist

These are the images and clips that would make the repository stronger as a portfolio piece.

## Highest Value

- A clean three-quarter hero photo of the full V10 robot on a plain surface.
- A top-down photo showing the central electronics cage, PCB, receiver, battery wiring, and servo harness routing.
- A CAD render or screenshot from the same three-quarter angle as the physical photo.
- A short GIF or video of stand-to-stow movement.
- A short GIF or video of body tilt mode responding to stick input.

## Helpful Detail Shots

- Close-up of one leg showing the linkage, rods, pivots, and servo placement.
- Close-up of the PCB installed in the V10 body.
- CAD screenshot with one leg isolated.
- Isaac Sim / USD import screenshot, even if it shows the simulation limitation.
- Wiring diagram or annotated photo of the electronics path.

## Nice To Have

- Side-by-side image: old SpotMicro build vs V10 custom build.
- Before/after image showing the packaging change from messy wiring to the central electronics cage.
- One photo of the RC transmitter setup.
- One photo showing the robot safely supported during calibration.
